## 📋 Role Overview

This role is within the **AWS Bedrock Customer Success Engineering Escalations and Engagements Team (CSET-ENE)**. It is a **customer-facing senior IC role** that engages customers at their most difficult moments. The SA is expected to:

- Take **ambiguous, partially-defined business problems** and return with a design, a working system, and an adopted mechanism
- Produce fixes that benefit the **entire customer base**, not just the one in front of you
- **Build software** as a first-class deliverable (investigation platforms, agentic systems, pipelines, validators)
- Own platforms **end-to-end**, including credentials, cost controls, data provenance, and secure defaults
- Communicate to a **publishable bar** — RCAs, design docs, mass communications, training

---

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities

### 🔥 Customer Engagements & Escalations
- Lead the highest-impact escalations where **customer trust is already damaged**
- Own root cause resolution — cross-team investigation, naming the bug, getting it prioritized
- De-escalate difficult situations with empathy and precision

### 🏗️ Architecture & Enablement
- Consult on customer architectures with trade-off judgment (extensibility, scalability, cost)
- Influence product roadmap on behalf of customers
- Author **migration guides, best-practice guides, blogs, workshops**

### 💬 Communication
- Author RCAs, customer wording, mass communications
- Engage senior technical leaders and mid-level business executives
- Write narratives and design docs; speak at events

### 🛠️ Building & Mechanisms
- Design → write design doc → write code → ship → operate (in that order)
- Build for reuse: installable, documented, tested
- Codify expertise into **investigation patterns, guardrails, validators, knowledge pipelines**
- Know **when NOT to use agentic/AI** — judgment is the bar

### ⚙️ Process & Force Multiplication
- Replace manual toil with mechanisms (escalation flow, known-issues review, knowledge ingestion)
- Train AWS Support Operations, TAMs, service engineers, and customers
- Mentor and develop others; provide SA promotion assessments

### 🎯 The Bar Raiser Factor

Every Amazon loop has a **Bar Raiser** — a trained, independent interviewer from another org with veto power. Their job is to ensure the candidate raises the bar vs. 50% of current employees in the role.

For this specific role, expect the Bar Raiser to probe:
- **Judgment** — "When did you *not* use the shiny new technology, and why?"
- **Systemic thinking** — "How did your solution prevent the problem from recurring?"
- **Scalable impact** — "Who benefited from your work beyond the immediate customer/team?"
- **Writing quality** — "Walk me through something you've written to a publishable standard"
